Open Data Attribution & Citation Standards

Data utilized in this platform is sourced from official public domain registries and open-access scientific services. All incident and telemetry data is attributed to the respective Government of Nepal authorities (NDRRMA, DHM, DoR via BIPAD Portal under open government data guidelines). Global multi-hazard alerts are credited to UN OCHA / EC JRC GDACS. River discharge forecasts are produced by Copernicus GloFAS via Open-Meteo (licensed under CC BY 4.0). Satellite damage assessments are provided by the European Union Copernicus Emergency Management Service under the Copernicus Open Access Policy.

Nepal Disaster Watch

Nepal Disaster Watch is an independent open-source initiative built for the WebMCP developer ecosystem. It normalises and cross-references data published by Nepal and international authorities. It issues no official warnings and sends no legal alerts.

Official Data Portals & APIs

  • DHM Hydrology Nepal
