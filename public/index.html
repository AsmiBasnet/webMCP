Disaster zone declared · three months · 26 August 2026

768 people are dead. 2,502 are still missing.

On the morning of 26 August a glacier collapsed on Langtang Lirung and sent a wall of ice, water and rock down 72 kilometres of the Trishuli. It reached the border in seven minutes. This page is what to do about it — whether you are living through it, searching for someone, or trying to help from somewhere else.

If water is rising where you are

Move uphill, not downstream, and go now rather than gathering possessions — this flood travelled at roughly 50 metres per second and reached the border crossing seven minutes after the ice fell. Do not attempt to cross a flooded road or bridge on foot or by vehicle. Two barrier lakes formed upstream during this event; one was assessed as a breach risk before it drained.

02

What happened, and how large it is के भयो

Roughly 0.2 km² of ice broke from Langtang Lirung and fell about 1.2 kilometres, melting on impact. The tremor registered Ms 5.2 on seismometers worldwide. A second landslide followed three hours later. Seven districts along the Trishuli were hit: Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading, Gorkha, Tanahun, Nawalparasi and Chitwan.

What was destroyed

  • 41 bridges and 42 km of road, including the entire Betrawati–Rasuwagadhi corridor linking Nepal to Tibet.
  • 14 hydropower projects damaged; 431 MW taken off the national grid.
  • 20 schools across three districts, and 300+ vehicles swept from the customs yards.
  • On the Chinese side of the border: 16 dead and 546 missing near Gyirong.

03

What you are entitled to तपाईंले पाउने सहयोग

The government has declared the affected area a disaster zone for three months. These are the supports announced so far. Ask for them by name at your ward office.

1
Free medical treatment if you were injured घाइतेहरूलाई निःशुल्क उपचार
Announced for all people injured in this flood. Take yourself or the injured person to the nearest health facility and state that the injury is from the Bhote Koshi flood — do not wait to arrange payment first.
2
Financial assistance if your family lost someone मृतकका परिवारलाई आर्थिक सहायता
Claimed through your ward office and the District Administration Office. You will need the death or missing-person record — which is why filing it formally (section 04) matters even when everyone in the village already knows.
3
A place in a relief camp राहत शिविरमा स्थान
Camps are open at Trishuli Bazar (Nuwakot) and in northern Kathmandu, where a monastery is sheltering roughly 500 people. Five emergency camps were established in the first week. Your ward office or the nearest army or police post will direct you to the closest one with space.
4
The rehabilitation package, when it lands पुनर्स्थापना प्याकेज
The Ministry of Finance has been directed to prepare a relief and rehabilitation package for affected individuals and businesses. It was not published as of 30 August. Register your losses at the ward office now so you are on the list when it opens — retroactive registration is usually harder.

Register your losses even if nothing is being given out yet

Every compensation scheme that follows a Nepali disaster is paid against the official damage assessment. The assessment is being compiled now. A household that is not in it when it closes spends the following year trying to get added.

04

Looking for someone हराएका व्यक्तिको खोजी

2,502 people are out of contact, 589 of them foreign nationals. There is a chain for this, and it only works if you go through it in order.

1
Start at the ward office वडा कार्यालयबाट सुरु गर्नुहोस्
The ward office of the missing person's home address. This is where the local list is compiled and escalated upward.
2
Then the District Administration Office and District Police Office जिल्ला प्रशासन तथा प्रहरी कार्यालय
Outside the Kathmandu valley you can reach your district centre by dialling 1234 from anywhere in Nepal.
3
File the formal record with Nepal Police नेपाल प्रहरीमा आधिकारिक विवरण दर्ता
Call 100. This is the record that search operations, identification and later compensation are all run against.

A village list or a group chat is not a record

Names circulating on WhatsApp, Viber and Facebook are not searched against and do not entitle a family to anything. In Nuwakot the army posted printed lists at relief centres and families gathered around them — those postings reflect the official record, they do not replace filing one. If you have only told neighbours, the state does not yet know your relative is missing.

05

How to help, lawfully कसरी सहयोग गर्ने

Nepal is running this response under a one-door policy. Read the box below before you donate anywhere, because it changes what you are allowed to do.

The one-door policy — this is not optional

The Government of Nepal has not granted permission to any private organisation, group or individual to collect relief funds or materials independently for this disaster. Independent fundraising is unlawful. All cash contributions go to the Prime Minister's Disaster Relief Fund; material donations go to one of three designated hubs, or are coordinated directly with the Chief District Officer of the affected district.

There is no cap on donations. The stated purpose is to prevent duplication and diversion, and to get supplies moving through a single logistics chain rather than forty competing ones.

Give money

Prime Minister's Disaster Relief Fund
The official and only lawful destination for cash. Accepts Visa and MasterCard from anywhere in the world, plus connectIPS, eSewa, AliPay+, UPI-India, and Fonepay or NepalPay QR. Donations can be made in NPR or USD. For large amounts that a card issuer declines, the fund advises a SWIFT transfer through your own bank.

How to tell a real appeal from a fake one

  • Anyone soliciting for this disaster who is not the PM's fund or an established international agency is, at minimum, operating without permission. That is the clearest test available right now.
  • A real appeal names the legal entity behind it, not just a cause. A personal bank account, an eSewa number in an individual's name, or a crypto wallet is not an appeal.
  • Fundraising fraud around this flood was reported within 72 hours of the water. Urgency and a countdown are the standard pressure tactics — a genuine appeal will still be there in an hour.
  • Reverse-image search the photographs. Recycled disaster imagery from 2024 and from Pakistan is already circulating attached to this event.

The record has not caught up with the disaster

Nepal's official machine-readable incident database, BIPAD, logged 10 deaths nationwide across the seven days spanning this flood. The Nepal Police bulletin for the same period reports 768. Both are government sources; the first is simply not the one that gets updated during an emergency. If you are making decisions from a dashboard, know which of those two it is reading.
